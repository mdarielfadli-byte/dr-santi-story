Publish 2-4 articles each month. Drafts retain Dr Santi's reflective, warm, practical voice. SEO adjustment happens after drafting: select one search intent, refine title/H1/meta description, add descriptive H2s, internal links, and a relevant CTA.

## On-page checklist for every article

1. One main keyword in title, H1, URL, intro, and one H2 only when natural.
2. Use Dr Santi's own examples and phrasing; do not force keyword repetition.
3. Add 2-3 internal links: relevant service page, another article, and Contact CTA.
4. Add a 140-160-character meta description with reader benefit.
5. Add author byline, publication date, image alt text, and Article schema after publishing.
