# Cycle 1 execution plan

This plan translates the active tracker notes into work that can be operated across Dr Santi's Story, BangunBrand, and technical delivery.

## 1. Website and content system

**Technical owner: Deriel**

- Publish the Leadership in the Home pathway: Home section, dedicated landing page, Stories entry point, sitemap, and editable CMS page.
- Replace remaining approved visual placeholders and review card crops at desktop and mobile breakpoints.
- Keep CMS as the draft/review space. The Dr Santi team edits copy; technical delivery checks preview and publishes approved changes.
- Add parent or audience questions to event pages only after the team supplies the approved question list and consent/status for publishing it.

**Inputs required from Dr Santi / BangunBrand**

- Approval of the Leadership in the Home messaging and the final photo selection.
- Final photos and captions for Home, About, Speaking, events, and articles.
- Approved questions from Fantasia and Cartea, including whether names/identifiers must be omitted.

## 2. SEO operations

**Technical owner: Deriel**

- Maintain crawlability, canonical URLs, sitemap, Schema, and CMS article/event rendering.
- Use the keyword bank to create content briefs and track index coverage, impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, organic users, downloads, and inquiries.
- Report a baseline after 28 days and a monthly comparison thereafter.

**Success measures**

- 100% of approved core pages indexable and present in the sitemap.
- Publishing cadence and quality: each article has a focused keyword, title, meta description, featured image, alt text, internal links, CTA, and publish date.
- Growth targets are set only after the first 28-day Search Console baseline. No traffic guarantee is made before that baseline exists.

## 3. Funnel and CRM

**Technical owner: Deriel**

- Keep inquiry and Reading Ritual submissions working and document the ownership of the receiving Apps Script/CRM workflow.
- Test forms quarterly and record a clear success or failure state.

**Inputs required from the owner**

- Confirm the form inbox/CRM owner and the response SLA.
- Share the appropriate viewer/editor access to the team; do not distribute passwords or API tokens.

## 4. Campaign landing pages and activation

**Technical owner: Deriel**

- Build or update landing pages, event documentation, RSVP/inquiry flow, QR code, download flow, and UTM measurement.
- Keep Fantasia and Cartea as event documentation pages; use separate campaign landing pages when an event needs registration or conversion.

**Inputs required from Dr Santi / BangunBrand**

- Event objective, audience, date, location, partner, CTA, approved copy, approved visual assets, and question/FAQ material.

## 5. Paid media readiness

**Technical owner: Deriel after approval**

- Prepare media plan, landing page, tracking, creative test matrix, campaign setup, weekly optimisation, and baseline report.
- Report spend, impressions, reach, CPM, CTR, CPC, landing-page views, leads, CPL, and assisted Instagram/profile growth where measurable.

**Decisions required before launch**

- Primary conversion: inquiry, event RSVP, 7 Days download, or awareness.
- Budget, campaign duration, target geography, audience, approved channel, creative owner, and lead follow-up owner.
- If paid media is outside Instagram, approve the channel (for example Google Search, YouTube, Display, or partner media) and the path from ad to website/Instagram.

## 6. Out of technical delivery scope

BangunBrand / SDP should own ongoing guest-star outreach, partnership and revenue pipeline, event meetups and relationship management, day-to-day social editorial, and LinkedIn thought-leadership execution. Technical delivery supports these through landing pages, tracking, media-kit pages, documentation, and conversion reporting.
