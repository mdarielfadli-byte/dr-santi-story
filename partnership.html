Partnership

A library is more than a collection. It is an everyday invitation.

Dr Santi's Story partners with homes, schools, and workplaces to shape thoughtful reading environments—starting from the people who will use them.

01 · Home library

A reading corner that grows with a family.

Guidance on the collection, routines, and conversation prompts that help books feel close at home.

  • Reading profile & collection direction
  • Age-appropriate title curation
  • Family reading ritual guide

02 · School library

A library that invites students to return.

Curated pathways that connect shelves, classroom practice, and a living culture of reading.

  • Collection and category review
  • Reading culture activation
  • Educator workshop options

03 · Workplace library

A shared shelf for thinking together.

Reading collections and conversation formats that support learning, perspective, and workplace culture.

  • Theme-based book curation
  • Library corner direction
  • Book conversation facilitation
