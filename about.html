About Dr Santi

Learning becomes meaningful when it helps us see more clearly.

Approved portrait
of Dr Santi

Santi Dharmaputra, PhD, is a learning and leadership practitioner with experience in education and corporate sectors across Indonesia, Australia, and the Netherlands. Her work has touched on language, law, research, executive search, and leadership development.

With a PhD in Sociology and Language from the University of Sydney, she brings curiosity and research into practical conversations about how people learn, grow, and lead.

Through Dr Santi’s Story, she shares perspectives on learning, reading, and leadership with families, educators, and organisations.
